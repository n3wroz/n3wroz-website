N3WROZ Interactive / About us

Turning ideas into useful experiences.

N3WROZ Interactive is an independent developer brand focused on software development and digital product design. Our goal is to create products that make technology more understandable, useful and accessible.

Who we are

N3WROZ Interactive is an independently operated software development brand. We do not claim to be a large company or a large team. Instead, we approach ideas through thoughtful planning, testing on real devices and improvement based on user feedback.

Individual developer: Harun Evik.

Our first featured product is POFRA Media Player. Over time, we aim to develop software for different needs and offer those products under the same brand.

Our principles

How we work

01

Put users first

03
