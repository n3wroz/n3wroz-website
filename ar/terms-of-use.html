4. USER RESPONSIBILITIES

POFRA Connect lets Android phones, tablets, and Android TV devices act as sender or receiver on the same local Wi-Fi network. The receiving device displays its own temporary QR code and six-digit PIN (valid for up to 10 minutes and refreshable); the sender scans it and transfers a media URL and title. The receiving device plays the media directly from the source URL; the file itself is not uploaded to the app server. Only connect devices you own or are authorized to use. Keep the QR code and PIN private. Google Cast may send supported media links to compatible receivers under Google's and the receiver's terms. POFRA Connect transfers media links, not the entire phone or computer screen.
